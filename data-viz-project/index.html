Is Arsenal's Premier League Title Belief Growing?

A closer look at Arsenal's record and 2025-26 season statistics through Matchweek 12

By Riley Bomgardner

November 23, 2025

As North London side Arsenal sit six points clear at the top of the table, fans are left wondering if this will finally be the year Arsenal win the league again. The Gunners have not achieved Premier League Champion status since the 2003-04 season, but with incredible teamwork and unbelievable stats this sesason, the hope lives on.
